26 — MEMORY.md: the notes it keeps on you

There is a second file, and you do not write this one — it does. Every session starts with an empty head, so it takes notes to carry across.

CLAUDE.md

You write it. Instructions and facts about the machine. Read at startup, every session.

MEMORY.md

It writes it, in ~/.claude/projects/…/memory/. What it learned from you: your preferences, your corrections, what this work is about.

Correct it once:

> don't tell me whether a mapping rate is good. Give me the number and the file you read it from. ● Understood. Saved 1 memory

Tomorrow, in a new session, without being asked:

Recalled 1 memory ● 76.58 % uniquely mapped — star_out/ALK_L_Z/Log.final.out

/memory shows what it saved. Go and read it — it is plain markdown, and yours to edit or delete.

A record of how you work — not your declaration. That is ai_log.md.

28 — Ask for measurements, not conclusions

Don't

  • “Is this sample good?”
  • “Is HMA4 upregulated?”
  • “Which tool should I trust?”

It will answer all three. Confidently. Without evidence you can check.

Do

  • “Print the per-base quality summary.”
  • “Give me the HMA4 count from all three matrices.”
  • “What would distinguish those two explanations?”

Now you have something you can verify, and the judgement stayed yours.

Every one of the five failures came from asking for a conclusion where a measurement was available.

This is a rule for this course, not for life. Outside it you will set a goal and let an agent run — that is what the tool is for, and it is how most of the work is done. What decides whether that is safe is whether you could catch a wrong answer if one came back. Here the judgement is the thing being examined, so it has to be yours. Later it is yours to delegate — once you can tell a measurement from a verdict without being told which is which.

18 Sep

29 — Cognitive debt

Which lane you are in is settled by one question: could you tell if the answer were wrong? When you can, hand it over — that is what the tool is for. In this course you cannot yet, so every step you accept without following it is borrowed.

the repayment, cheap while you are still there Ask while it works — why that flag · what breaks if I drop it. Then read any agent-written command back in your own words.
You are here to learn, not to deliver — and on 6 October there is no agent in the room.
18 Sep

30 — What you are graded on

  • Not the typing. The agent types faster than all of us.
  • Not whether you used AI. You are expected to — and to declare it.

You are graded on whether you can say what the numbers mean and why you believe them.

In practice: the “checked” line in your ai_log.md, and your ability to defend a number on 7 October when someone asks where it came from.

31 — Five lines to keep

  1. A very fast typist. Not a biologist.
  2. What it reads leaves the machine. Public data only.
  3. Tell it where it is. Most failures are missing premises (context).
  4. Ask for measurements, not conclusions.
  5. Declare it. The “checked” line is the one that counts.
